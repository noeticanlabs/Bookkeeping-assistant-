# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and open to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- give technical criticism clearly and respectfully;
- welcome questions and good-faith disagreement;
- distinguish demonstrated facts from assumptions and opinions;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption.

## Technical disagreement

This project encourages adversarial testing of software and architecture. Adversarial testing is not adversarial treatment of contributors. Challenge claims, tests, threat models, and implementations rigorously while treating people professionally.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces managed on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions or participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Serious safety, harassment, or security concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner or appropriate project maintainer rather than escalated publicly.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and is informed by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant, while using project-specific language.
